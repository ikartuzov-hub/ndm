Tap in Segurança Social Direta

It let you through? Done.

Check the address, tap «Enviar pedido». The card comes by post in one to two weeks. Flying sooner? Same place: Ações → «Obter certificado provisório». Instant PDF, same legal value as the card.

2b

Refused? That's your old permit in their database.

This sentence means Segurança Social can't see your current residence permit. You weren't refused the card — the form refused.
