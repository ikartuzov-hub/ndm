Booking a trail in Madeira: residents and visitors in one request

Since 2026 every classified trail is booked on SIMplifica. Residents don't pay, but they still book. The whole route, with no portal account, and the traps we ran into.

Book — step 1 of 7

The card doesn't get you onto the trail. The booking does.

The resident card makes the ticket free, but the ticket is still required for every outing. The booking is how they control how many people are on a trail at once.

Booking in seven steps

1

Open the service — no account

The service is called «Pagamento de Taxas para acesso aos Percursos Pedestres Classificados». You don't need to log in to SIMplifica: we did the whole booking without registering.

Open the booking on SIMplifica
2

Trail and time

At the bottom of the page, the «Ver disponibilidade» block, field «Percursos Pedestres» — type part of the name, for example «Balc» for PR 11. «Período da reserva» opens a week in 30-minute slots: pink ones are full, and you can pick only one slot. «Confirmar», then «Solicitar».

08:3009:0009:3010:00

Hover over a slot: the portal shows free places for residents, visitors and operators separately.

3

Email and two confirmations

Enter your email — the tickets go there. «Solicitar NIF para faturação» only if you need an invoice with a tax number. Of the three boxes, two are mandatory: personal responsibility on the trail and the refund rule. The third is only for people with a disability above 60 %.

4

How many tickets

The same screen has two blocks, «Não residentes» and «Residentes», each with adults, children up to 12 and disability cases. Residents and visitors fit in one request — no need to book separately.

5

Each resident's NIF

At «Inserir NIF residentes» the portal asks for one NIF per resident. That is how it checks residency: no login, no card number.

6

Review — and say «no» to the extra booking

The portal shows the trail and time again and asks whether you want to add another booking to the request.

The trap: a second booking

Say «yes» by accident and the request holds two bookings — you pay twice. We fell for it: one visitor, 9.00 € instead of 4.50 €. Say «no» unless you really want a second trail.

7

Summary and payment

The last screen shows the amount and the payment deadline. Press «Resumo» first and check everything, then «Pagar». Tickets and invoice arrive by email.

On the day

  • Bring the ticket, the resident card and a photo ID.
  • Check on the IFCN site that the trail is open — they often close after rain. Entering a closed trail costs a 250 € fine; no ticket, 50 €.
  • Once paid, you can reschedule or get a refund only if IFCN closes the trail: helpdesk.ifcn@madeira.gov.pt. The Contact Center cannot change bookings.
  • Got a «reserva cancelada por inatividade» email but you have tickets and an invoice? It refers to an earlier attempt left halfway — your booking is valid.
